AMPUTATION — FIELD MANAGEMENT

IMMEDIATE: STOP THE BLEEDING
1. Apply tourniquet 2–3 inches above the amputation site.
2. Tighten until bleeding stops.
3. Record time of application (write on skin if no paper).
4. Do NOT remove once applied.

IF NO TOURNIQUET AVAILABLE
- Pack wound tightly with clean cloth. Apply firm direct pressure over packed wound.
- Maintain constant pressure.

THE AMPUTATED PART
- Wrap in clean moist cloth or gauze.
- Place inside a clean waterproof bag (plastic bag works).
- Keep cool — place bag near ice or cold water, not directly on ice.
- Keep part with person at all times.

SHOCK PREVENTION
- Lay person flat, raise legs if no other injury.
- Keep warm. Talk calmly.
- Do NOT give food or drink.

SIGNS OF DETERIORATION TO MONITOR
- Bleeding through bandage (add more material, increase pressure).
- Pale, cold, clammy skin; confusion; rapid breathing = shock.
- Send someone for any available medical help immediately.
