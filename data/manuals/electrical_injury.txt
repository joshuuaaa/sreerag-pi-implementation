ELECTRICAL INJURY — FIELD MANAGEMENT

DO NOT TOUCH VICTIM UNTIL POWER IS OFF
- Turn off power at the source (switch, breaker).
- If power cannot be turned off: push the person free using a DRY wooden object (broom, chair leg). Never use metal or wet material.
- Do not become a second victim.

LIGHTNING STRIKE
- Lightning victims are NOT electrically charged — safe to touch immediately.
- Begin CPR without delay.

ELECTRICAL BURNS
- Entry wound (often looks small) + exit wound on opposite side of the body.
- Internal damage along conduction path can be massive even when entry wound is tiny.
- Cover surface burns with a clean dry dressing. Do NOT use wet dressings.

CARDIAC ARREST RISK
- Electrical current can cause cardiac arrest at any point.
- Check breathing and pulse.
- If not breathing normally: start CPR immediately.
- Be prepared to continue CPR — electrical victims can recover after prolonged resuscitation.

MONITOR
- Even if conscious and walking: hidden injuries, cardiac arrhythmia, and delayed muscle breakdown are serious risks.
- Keep lying flat, monitor breathing and pulse every 5 minutes.
- Watch for confusion, chest pain, seizures, or passage of dark/red urine (muscle breakdown).

Seek medical care as soon as reachable.
