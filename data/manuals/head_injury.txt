HEAD INJURY — FIELD MANAGEMENT

IMMEDIATE PRIORITIES
1. Airway: if unconscious, tilt head back gently (jaw thrust if spinal injury suspected).
2. Breathing: check breathing. If not breathing normally: CPR.
3. Bleeding: apply firm pressure around (NOT over) any skull deformity.
4. Spine: assume spinal injury with any forceful head trauma. DO NOT move.

CONCUSSION SIGNS (MILD BRAIN INJURY)
- Confusion, dazed appearance.
- Brief loss of consciousness.
- Memory gap around the event.
- Headache, dizziness, nausea, light sensitivity.
Action: keep them resting. No activity. Monitor closely.

SERIOUS BRAIN INJURY SIGNS (CRITICAL)
- Repeated vomiting.
- Worsening or severe headache.
- Unequal pupils or one very large pupil.
- Clear fluid from nose or ears (battle’s sign — bruising behind ear).
- Seizure after head injury.
- Confusion that worsens.
- Loss of consciousness lasting more than a few seconds.

NO EMBEDDED OBJECTS
- Do NOT remove objects embedded in the skull.
- Apply pressure around the object, not on it.
- Cover with ring-shaped dressing or improvise.

POSITION
- Conscious: lying flat or slight elevation of head.
- Unconscious but breathing: recovery position (protect neck).
- Vomiting: recovery position.

MONITOR EVERY 10 MINUTES
- Responsiveness, pupils, breathing, speech.
- Deterioration = brain compression. Send for help immediately.
