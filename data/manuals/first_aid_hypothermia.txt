FIRST AID: HYPOTHERMIA (COLD EXPOSURE)

Goal: warm the CORE and prevent further heat loss.
Assume help may be delayed.

IMMEDIATE ACTIONS
1) Get out of wind and wet.
2) Remove wet clothing. Dry skin.
3) Insulate: blanket, sleeping bag, coats, dry clothes.
4) Warm the core: chest, back, neck, groin.

DO NOT
- Do not rub or massage skin.
- Do not warm arms/legs first in severe hypothermia.
- No alcohol.

IF AWAKE AND CAN SWALLOW
- Warm sweet drinks in small sips.
- Keep them lying down and calm.

SEVERE WARNING SIGNS (TREAT AS CRITICAL)
- Confusion, slurred speech, stumbling.
- Not shivering or very sleepy.
- Slow, shallow breathing.

IF UNCONSCIOUS
- Handle gently. Keep flat.
- Check breathing for 10 seconds.
- If not breathing normally: start CPR.

MONITOR
- Re-check every 5 minutes for alertness and breathing.
- Seek medical care when reachable; severe hypothermia can worsen later.
