FIRST AID: HEAT EXHAUSTION / HEAT STROKE

Heat illness can kill quickly. Cooling is the priority.

HEAT STROKE WARNING SIGNS (CRITICAL)
- Confusion, collapse, seizure, unconsciousness.
- Very hot skin; may be dry or still sweating.

IMMEDIATE COOLING (DO THIS NOW)
1) Move to shade.
2) Loosen/remove extra clothing.
3) Cool aggressively: pour cool water on skin and fan hard.
4) Put cool wet cloths on neck, armpits, and groin.

FLUIDS
- Only if fully awake and able to swallow.
- Small sips frequently.
- Oral rehydration: 1 liter clean water + 6 level teaspoons sugar + 1/2 teaspoon salt.

DO NOT
- Do not force fluids if vomiting, confused, or drowsy.

MONITOR
- Improvement should begin within 10–20 minutes of cooling.
- If not improving or mental status changes: treat as heat stroke and keep cooling.
- Seek medical care when reachable.
