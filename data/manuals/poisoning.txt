POISONING — FIELD MANAGEMENT

FIRST: YOUR SAFETY
- Do NOT enter a toxic environment without protection.
- Move person to fresh air if inhalation poisoning.

CHECK AIRWAY AND BREATHING
- If unconscious: open airway, check breathing.
- If not breathing: CPR.
- If unconscious but breathing: recovery position.

INGESTED POISON (SWALLOWED)
- Do NOT induce vomiting — causes more damage with corrosives and hydrocarbons.
- If CONSCIOUS and able to swallow: rinse mouth with water, spit out.
- Do NOT give milk or activated charcoal unless instructed by medical professional.
- Note the substance name and amount if known — vital for treatment.

CHEMICAL ON SKIN
- Remove contaminated clothing — protect your own skin.
- Flush with large volumes of clean water for 20 minutes.
- Do not try to neutralise chemicals.

INHALED POISON / FUMES
- Move to fresh air. Open windows if enclosed space.
- Fresh air only — do not give oxygen unless medically trained.

SPECIFIC DISASTER POISONINGS
- Contaminated water: rehydrate ONLY with known safe water. ORS if tolerated.
- Carbon monoxide (generator/fire): fresh air, monitor breathing, rest.
- Organophosphate (pesticide): SLUDGE signs (salivation, lacrimation, urination, diarrhoea, vomiting) = extreme emergency. Seek antidote (atropine) medically.

Record all details about the substance for when medical care is reached.
