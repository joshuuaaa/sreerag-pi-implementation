FIRST AID: SEIZURE

During the seizure, your job is safety.

DO
- Clear hard objects away.
- Cushion the head.
- Loosen tight clothing at neck.
- Time the seizure.

DO NOT
- Do not hold them down.
- Do not put anything in their mouth.

AFTER SHAKING STOPS
- Roll onto their side (recovery position).
- Check breathing.
- Expect confusion/sleepiness.

CRITICAL
- Seizure lasts 5 minutes or more.
- Repeated seizures without waking.
- Trouble breathing or injury.
These need urgent medical care when reachable.
