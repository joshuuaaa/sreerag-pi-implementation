AIRWAY BLOCKAGE / CHOKING

CONSCIOUS AND CAN COUGH
- Encourage forceful coughing. Do NOT intervene.
- If coughing fails to clear it, move to back blows.

CONSCIOUS BUT CANNOT BREATHE OR SPEAK
1. Lean them forward. Give 5 firm back blows with heel of hand between shoulder blades.
2. If object not cleared: 5 abdominal thrusts. Stand behind them. Fist above navel, pull sharply IN and UP.
3. Alternate back blows and abdominal thrusts until cleared or they collapse.

UNCONSCIOUS AFTER CHOKING
- Lower to the floor carefully.
- Start CPR. Before rescue breaths: look in mouth, remove visible object.
- Do NOT do blind finger sweeps.
- Send someone for any available help while you work.

INFANT (under 1 year)
- 5 back blows face-down on forearm.
- 5 chest thrusts (not abdominal).
- Alternate until cleared.

AFTER CLEARING
- Seat them and keep calm.
- Tell them to cough fully.
- Watch for worsening breathing or swelling — internal bruising possible.
- Seek medical care when reachable.
