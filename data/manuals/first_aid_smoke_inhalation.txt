FIRST AID: SMOKE INHALATION / CARBON MONOXIDE

Priority: fresh air and breathing.

IMMEDIATE
1) Move to fresh air. Get away from smoke source.
2) Loosen clothing around neck/chest.
3) Sit upright or at 45 degrees to ease breathing.

AIRWAY BURN WARNING SIGNS (CRITICAL)
- Soot around nose/mouth.
- Hoarse voice.
- Burns to face/mouth.
Swelling can worsen quickly.

CARBON MONOXIDE CLUES
- Headache, nausea, dizziness.
- Multiple people sick in same area.
Action: get everyone into fresh air. Do not re-enter.

IF NOT BREATHING NORMALLY
- Open airway and start CPR.

MONITOR
- Check breathing every 2 minutes.
- Seek oxygen/medical care as soon as reachable.
