POSTPARTUM HAEMORRHAGE (PPH) — FIELD MANAGEMENT

WHEN IT OCCURS: Heavy bleeding within 24 hours of birth. A normal birth loses up to 500 ml. PPH = more than 500 ml or any bleed causing deterioration.

IMMANENT ACTIONS
1. UTERINE MASSAGE:
   - Place one hand flat on lower abdomen (above pubic bone).
   - Massage with firm circular motion until uterus firms up (feels like a cricket ball).
   - Do NOT stop, even briefly.
2. BREASTFEED (if possible): stimulates oxytocin, causing the uterus to contract.
3. Encourage the woman to urinate: full bladder prevents uterus contracting.

IF UTERUS REMAINS SOFT AND BLEEDING CONTINUES
- Bimanual compression (if trained): one hand inside (or externally on front of uterus) and one hand on back. Compress firmly.
- Pack the vagina with clean cloth if bleeding is severe and uterus will not firm.

SHOCK MANAGEMENT
- Lay flat.
- Keep warm.
- Do NOT give food or fluids by mouth if confused.
- Monitor breathing, pulse, and skin colour every 5 minutes.
- Send someone for any available medical help immediately.

RISK FACTORS
- Prolonged labour, multiple pregnancy, large baby, previous PPH.
- Any of these = increased vigilance needed post-delivery.

CRITICAL: PPH kills within minutes. Uterine massage and shock treatment are the only field options. Medical care is needed as urgently as possible.
