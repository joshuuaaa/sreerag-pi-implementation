FIRST AID: DEHYDRATION / DIARRHEA

In disasters, dehydration kills faster than hunger.

SIGNS
- Thirst, dry mouth, headache.
- Dizziness, weakness.
- Dark urine or not urinating.
- Confusion or fainting (critical).

ORAL REHYDRATION SOLUTION (ORS)
- 1 liter clean water
- 6 level teaspoons sugar
- 1/2 teaspoon salt
Stir until dissolved.

HOW TO GIVE
- Small sips every minute.
- If vomiting: 1 teaspoon every 1–2 minutes.
- Keep trying; stopping fluids makes it worse.

DO NOT
- Do not force fluids if unconscious or cannot swallow safely.

WHEN IT IS CRITICAL
- Confusion, unconsciousness.
- No urination for many hours.
- Cannot keep any fluid down.
These need medical care as soon as reachable.
